# Official Bell & Wyson BW-Pix+ Troubleshooting Manual

> This document is reproduced from the official user manual of the **BW-Pix+ / BW Pix**
> smart bulb camera (by Bell & Wyson, www.bellandwyson.com). BWPixAdapter is an
> independent, open-source Android client for this hardware. The following official
> guidance applies to the physical device itself.

---

## Troubleshooting

### Bulb cannot be connected to the network?

- Please make sure you are connected to the Internet.
- Please make sure your bulb is powered.
- If still not working please close and restart your BW-PIX+ app and then switch off
  and restart your bulb.
- When you restart your bulb you must wait at least 30 seconds to make sure that the
  bulb Wi-Fi module is active (the light is immediately working but the Wi-Fi module
  needs 30 seconds to re-boot).
- If still not working, please reboot the bulb by pressing the button "Reset",
  located close to the microphone, with a small pin until you hear the confirmation
  beep. Then start again the process of installation.

### Problem in installing the SD card?

- Please note that the SD card reader only supports from **2GB ~ 32GB**. A card above
  or below these size values won't work.
- When you insert or remove the SD card, make sure that the bulb is switched off
  (non-powered), otherwise you may seriously damage your SD card.

## Updates

Unless otherwise notified by Bell & Wyson, the updates are done via cloud service
without action required on your part. If you find that your bulb doesn't react
quickly enough, do not hesitate to switch off (power off) your BW-PIX+ and to close
the app and then wait about 30 seconds before use it again.
